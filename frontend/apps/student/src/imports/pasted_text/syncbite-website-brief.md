Design and build a polished, responsive student-facing website for a campus food prebooking platform called "SyncBite".

IMPORTANT:
This is an MVP for VIT Vellore campus food stalls.

Do not add unnecessary features.

The entire website should focus on only two primary actions:

1. ORDER NOW
2. SCHEDULE ORDER

The goal is to make ordering food extremely fast, visually attractive, and effortless for college students who want to avoid queues at campus restaurants and stalls.

---

# BRAND IDENTITY

Use the uploaded SyncBite logo as the primary brand logo.

The logo features:

- A stylized Indian food bowl/pot illustration
- A playful food-themed visual identity
- Dark navy/black typography
- Warm golden food illustration
- A clean but slightly funky personality

The website should feel like a modern food startup designed specifically for young college students.

Do not make it look corporate, generic, or overly similar to Swiggy/Zomato.

---

- Occasional green success indicators

# HOMEPAGE

Create a visually striking but fast-loading homepage.

## HERO SECTION

Use a large cinematic food-focused hero section.

Visual direction:

Dark navy background or high-quality food photography.

Feature:

- A steaming bowl of food
- Food stalls / burgers / pizza / campus meals
- SyncBite branded food packaging or logo
- Subtle floating hand-drawn illustrations

Do not use an overly large static hero image.

Instead, design the hero to support a paused 3D/video-style visual with subtle ambient animations.

Possible ambient animations:

- Steam gently moving
- Tiny food elements subtly moving
- Slow camera parallax
- Very subtle bag handle movement

Animations should be slow and lightweight.

Avoid scroll-triggered animations that make the website feel slower.

# PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

Design the interface to feel extremely fast.

Requirements:

- Lightweight visual hierarchy
- Minimal unnecessary animations
- Lazy-loaded food images
- Skeleton loaders instead of full-screen loading
- Avoid autoplay-heavy video backgrounds
- No excessive scroll animations
- Immediate button feedback
- Smooth transitions under 200ms where appropriate

The website should feel responsive even on average campus mobile internet.

---

- Cart count updates instantly
- CTA hover animations
- Restaurant cards gently lift on hover
- Smooth page transitions
- Small toast notification when food is added

Avoid flashy animations.
